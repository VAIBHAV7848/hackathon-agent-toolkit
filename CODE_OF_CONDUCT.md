# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in this project welcoming and constructive for
people with different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Accept corrections and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, licenses, attribution, and project boundaries.
- Share minimal reproductions and avoid exposing secrets or private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate
exposure of private information, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, close, or remove content that violates this policy
and may block participants from project spaces when necessary. Reports should
be sent privately using the channel described in `SECURITY.md` or directly to the
repository owner. Maintainership and enforcement decisions should be explained
when doing so does not compromise a reporter's safety or privacy.

This project adapts the goals of community conduct policies; it is not a claim
that the toolkit is affiliated with or certified by another organization.
